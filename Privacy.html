Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Life Count ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy while helping you make every day count for Christ. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information in our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect
  • Account Information: If you register an account, we may collect your email address, name, and basic profile details.
  • Journal Entries & Assignments: Your personal journal entries, assignments, and prayer logs are stored securely to synch across your devices.
  • Community Data: Information shared when connecting with friends (such as shared scripture notes or friend requests).
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics strictly used to improve app performance and stability.
2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide, support, and personalize the features of Life Count, including calculate age benchmarks, managing your journal entries, and enabling community features with your friends.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not share private journal content or personal faith goals with any third party.

4. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your data both in transit and at rest.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. You can request deletion directly within the app settings or by visiting our Support Page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@lifecountapp.com.
