Frequently Asked Questions

How is the target age (70-80) calculated?

You select your preferred target age range in settings based on Psalm 90:10. Life Count calculates remaining days, weeks, and months from your birth date.

Are my journal entries private?

Yes. Your daily journal entries are completely private to your account and are never shared with your connected friends unless explicitly shared by you.

How do I request deletion of my account?

You can delete your account under App Settings > Account > Delete Account, or send an email to support@lifecountapp.com with "Account Deletion" in the subject line.
